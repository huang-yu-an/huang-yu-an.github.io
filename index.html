研究方向Research Interests

单细胞组学与计算生物学

单细胞 RNA 测序（scRNA-seq）让我们得以在细胞分辨率下研究组织异质性、肿瘤微环境与发育轨迹。 提出了一系列用于细胞类型识别、细胞间通信推断、 药物响应预测的图神经网络与 Transformer 方法，发表在 Genome Biology、Communications Biology、PLoS Computational Biology 等期刊。

Single-cell Omics & Computational Biology

scRNA-seq reveals tissue heterogeneity, tumor microenvironment and developmental trajectories at cellular resolution. We develop GNNs and Transformers for cell-type identification, cell-cell communication inference, and drug-response prediction, published in Genome Biology, Communications Biology, PLoS Computational Biology, etc.

脑影像与神经退行性疾病

结合 fMRI 与单细胞数据，探索阿尔茨海默病等神经退行性疾病的早期诊断标志物。 代表工作 scBIT 把单细胞转录组信息融入 fMRI 预测框架（IEEE TMI 2026）。

Neuroimaging & Neurodegenerative Diseases

Integrating fMRI with single-cell data for early diagnostic biomarkers of Alzheimer's disease and other neurodegenerative disorders. scBIT fuses single-cell transcriptomics into fMRI-based prediction (IEEE TMI 2026).

生物医学知识图谱与链路预测

lncRNA–miRNA、circRNA–disease、miRNA–drug、TF–target gene 等生物实体之间相互作用预测， 是药物重定位与精准医疗的关键。基于异质图嵌入、图卷积与自编码器的方法体系。

Biomedical Knowledge Graphs & Link Prediction

Predicting interactions among lncRNAs, miRNAs, circRNAs, diseases, drugs and TFs is key to drug repurposing and precision medicine. Heterogeneous-graph-embedding, graph-convolution and autoencoder-based methods.

医学图像与可解释 AI

去偏表征、反事实推理与因果干预在医学影像中的应用。代表工作 Anti-Confounding Hashing（IEEE TNNLS 2025）与 CausalMixNet（Medical Image Analysis 2025）。

Medical Imaging & Explainable AI

Debiased representation, counterfactual reasoning, and causal intervention for medical image analysis. Representative works: Anti-Confounding Hashing (IEEE TNNLS 2025) and CausalMixNet (MIA 2025).
